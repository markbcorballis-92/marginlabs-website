Eligibility

Can we fund you?

Four things decide whether we can look at your business. If you are close but not quite there, apply anyway and we will tell you straight.

The minimum

What every
application needs.

These apply to every product. We fund businesses registered in the United States, wherever your customers are.

Can we fund you?
  • A registered companyAn LLC, corporation or partnership. We cannot fund sole proprietors yet.
  • Trading for a year or moreA full year shows us how your sales move through the seasons.
  • $5,000 a month in salesBelow that the numbers are too thin to read anything useful into.
  • A business bank accountOne we can read: connect it read-only, or send your latest six months of statements.
Sometimes we need more

Bigger asks
need a bit more.

For a cash advance, your bank on its own is usually enough. The other products, and anything large or unusual, need a little more. We tell you exactly what and why before you go looking for it, and we never ask for something we can find ourselves.

You sell wholesale What actually sold, not only what shipped. Sell-through and order data from your retailers, so we are not guessing at what moved.
You want a larger amount Your profit and loss, and a balance sheet. Above a certain size we need to see the whole business rather than one bank account.
You already have funding The agreements for anything you are still repaying, so we size around it rather than stack on top of it.
You are funding invoices or supplier bills Who owes you what and how long it has been, or the supplier invoices you want covered. Different products read different things.

If we do need something extra, you will hear it once, up front, rather than in a drip of requests over two weeks.
