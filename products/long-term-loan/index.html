Who it's for

Is this you?

You are funding something that pays back slowly: a fit-out, equipment, a move, a second site, buying someone out.

WholesaleYou sell in bulk to stockists and big-box buyers, then wait 30, 60, 90 days to be paid.
Bricks-and-mortarTills, footfall, and one season that has to pay for the whole year.
Direct-to-consumerYou sell through your own store and the money lands daily in small pieces.
How it works

Three things happen.

01
We look at the whole businessA longer commitment needs a fuller picture. Not only what came in this month, but whether the business can carry a fixed payment through a bad quarter.
02
You take a fixed amountOver an agreed term, at an agreed rate, with a schedule you can plan around.
03
You repay on scheduleThe same amount on the same day, whatever kind of month you have had. That is the trade you are making against a cash advance.
What we need

What you'd have to give us.

  • Two years of trading behind you
  • Your profit and loss, and a balance sheet
  • Six months of bank statements, or connect your bank
Worth knowing

This is the one product here that genuinely is a loan. It carries interest and a fixed repayment that does not flex when sales dip. If your revenue is lumpy or seasonal, a cash advance will treat you more kindly, and we will say so rather than sell you this.
