1 · What we do

MarginLabs is a principal funder to e-commerce and retail businesses. We fund businesses ourselves, from our own resources, through five products:

  • Merchant cash advance — a lump sum now, delivered back to us as an agreed share of your future sales.
  • Extended payment terms — we pay a supplier invoice for you, and you settle with us on an agreed date up to 120 days later.
  • Invoice financing — funding against invoices you have issued to your business customers, without your customers being told.
  • Line of credit — a limit you can draw on, repay and draw again.
  • Long-term loan — a fixed amount, repaid over a fixed term on a fixed schedule.

Not every product is available yet; a product's page on the site says whether it is. We will only offer you a product that is available, and only on the terms of a written agreement for that product. Before funding, we assess your business's finances, banking, store performance and digital presence to inform our decision.

2 · The nature of each product

The five products are not all the same kind of arrangement, and the difference matters:

  • A merchant cash advance is a purchase of a portion of your future receivables — it is not a loan. We pay you a purchase price today; in return you agree to deliver a fixed total amount (the “purchased amount”) from your future receipts. Because it is a purchase and not a loan, there is no interest rate and no fixed repayment schedule — remittance continues until the purchased amount has been delivered.
  • Invoice financing provides funds against specific invoices you have issued to other businesses. Your agreement will state whether it is structured as a purchase of those invoices or as an advance against them. It is confidential: we do not notify your customers, and you continue to collect payment from them.
  • Extended payment terms, a line of credit and a long-term loan are credit. You owe us what we have paid out on your behalf or lent to you, plus the agreed cost, on the agreed dates. A long-term loan carries interest and a fixed repayment that does not change with your sales.

For every product we are the principal: we are not a credit broker or intermediary, and we do not introduce you to, or arrange finance with, third-party lenders.

3 · How each product works

In every case the figures that bind you are the ones in the separate agreement for that product, which you sign. That agreement governs the funding itself; these website terms govern your use of the site and our assessment. In outline:

  • Merchant cash advance. We agree a purchase price (the amount we pay you now) and a purchased amount (the total you deliver from future receipts). You deliver an agreed percentage of your receipts (the “remittance rate”) until the purchased amount has been delivered in full. If your receipts run lower or higher than expected, a reconciliation may adjust remittances so they track your actual sales; the mechanics are set out in your agreement.
  • Extended payment terms. You tell us which supplier invoice to cover. We pay the supplier directly, and you pay us the invoice amount plus the agreed cost on an agreed date, up to 120 days after we paid.
  • Invoice financing. We advance an agreed proportion of the value of invoices you nominate. When your customer pays you, you pass the amount due to us under your agreement. If an invoice is disputed, reduced or goes unpaid, your agreement sets out what happens and what you owe.
  • Line of credit. We set a limit. You draw what you need, when you need it, and pay the agreed cost on what you have drawn. As you repay, that amount becomes available to draw again, for as long as the facility remains open under its agreement.
  • Long-term loan. We lend a fixed amount over an agreed term at an agreed rate. You repay in fixed installments on a fixed schedule, whatever your sales in a given month.
