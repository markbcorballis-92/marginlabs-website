1 · What we do

MarginLabs reviews an eCommerce or retail business's finances, banking, store performance and digital presence, and produces a “Read” — an assessment of funding-readiness with an improvement plan. Where you ask us to, we also help you obtain finance by introducing you to lenders and credit providers, and assisting with structure and negotiation.
