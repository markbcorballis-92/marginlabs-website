Your data, your call

Data Rights.

Last updated 12 September 2026 · Version 1.1

Your data belongs to you. Use this page to get a copy of what we hold, correct it, or ask us to delete it entirely. We act on every request within one month, free of charge.

What you can ask for

Access
A copy of all the personal and business data we hold about you.
Correction
Fix anything inaccurate or incomplete.
Deletion
Erase your data, including every document you uploaded.
Restriction / objection
Pause or stop us using your data in certain ways.
Portability
Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
Withdraw consent
Revoke any permission you gave us, for example to read your connected bank account.

How deletion works

When you ask us to delete your data, we:

  1. Verify it's really you (to protect your data from someone impersonating you).
  2. Permanently remove your uploaded documents from our encrypted storage.
  3. Delete your account and associated records from our database.
  4. Instruct our sub-processors to do the same where applicable.
  5. Confirm to you in writing once it's done.
One honest exception
If we have already funded you, a limited amount of information may need to be retained to meet legal, tax or regulatory duties (for example, anti-money-laundering records). We'll tell you exactly what, why, and for how long, and delete the rest.

Make a request

Email us from the address you used with us, and tell us:

  • your name and your business's name;
  • what you would like us to do: access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, portability, or withdrawing consent;
  • anything that helps us find your records.
Email your request privacy@getmarginlabs.com

How long it takes

We confirm receipt quickly and complete your request within one calendar month, as data protection law requires. If a request is especially complex we may extend by up to two further months, and we'll always tell you if so.
