What it costs

One fee.
Agreed before you sign.

You take an amount. You agree one total to pay back. That total is fixed before you sign anything and it does not move afterwards.

  • No interest rate, so nothing compounds while you repay
  • No fee for paying it off early
  • No arrangement fee, no late fee, no admin charges
  • The full cost in writing before you commit to anything

What it costs depends on which of the six you end up with, and on what your bank shows. A cash advance does not price like an invoice facility. We quote the real number once we have looked, and applying is free and puts you under no obligation.

Sometimes we need more

Bigger asks
need a bit more.

For a cash advance, your bank on its own is usually enough. The other products, and anything large or unusual, need a little more. We tell you exactly what and why before you go looking for it, and we never ask for something we can find ourselves.

You sell wholesale What actually sold, not only what shipped. Sell-through and order data from your stockists, so we are not guessing at what moved.
You want a larger amount Your profit and loss, and a balance sheet. Above a certain size we need to see the whole business rather than one bank account.
You already have funding The agreements for anything you are still repaying, so we size around it rather than stack on top of it.
You are funding invoices or supplier bills Who owes you what and how long it has been, or the supplier invoices you want covered. Different products read different things.

If we do need something extra, you will hear it once, up front, rather than in a drip of requests over a fortnight.

Being straight with you

We'll also tell you
what we couldn't see.

Every funder has gaps in what they can check. Most price the risk in quietly and say nothing about it. We show you the gaps, because they are usually the difference between a no and a bigger yes.

Nothing is auto-rejected. A no-for-now becomes a conversation.
