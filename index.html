Questions founders ask
Before you upload a single file.
Is it safe to share my bank statements and financials?+
Yes. Everything you upload is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in a private EU-hosted folder, and visible only to your named analyst. You can ask us to permanently delete all of it at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Data Rights page.
Do I have to pay to get started?+
No. Start with the free Borrowing Readiness Check — 90 seconds, no documents. The deeper Read-Lite is free too, built from your own numbers. You only pay if you choose to go further with a credit memo or a Sprint.
Do I need to give you access to my bank?+
Not to begin. The free Borrowing Readiness Check needs no documents at all. The deeper Read-Lite works from documents you upload (PDF or CSV), and direct bank or accounting connections are optional — only ever set up with your explicit consent later.
What makes you different from a finance broker?+
Brokers are paid by lenders on volume, so they're incentivised to place you in the biggest deal. We're paid by you, read your business like an underwriter first, and disclose any commission in writing — and we'll tell you when not to borrow.
Who actually reads my business?+
An ACA-qualified chartered accountant reads your file personally. You get findings in plain English, each tied to a number you can point to — never an opaque score.
